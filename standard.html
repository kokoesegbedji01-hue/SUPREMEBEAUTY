The Supreme Standard

A new standard for beauty.

The Supreme Standard is our framework for evaluating beauty products against scientific research, ingredient information, and the strength of the available evidence.

Our Goal

You should not need a chemistry degree to understand what you are putting on your body.

What It Means

The Supreme Standard is not designed to tell consumers what they must buy. It is designed to give consumers clearer information so they can make informed choices.

Evidence Matters

We distinguish between established evidence, potential concerns, emerging research, limited evidence, and context-dependent risks.

Transparent & Evolving

This methodology is public and is updated as scientific evidence changes.

Evidence Levels

Established (consistent, high-quality evidence) · Emerging (early or growing findings) · Limited (few or low-quality studies) · Context-dependent (risk varies by formula, amount, frequency, or person) · Potential concern (signals worth attention, not yet conclusive).

How We Evaluate

1. Identify the product's claims and ingredients. 2. Find peer-reviewed research on those ingredients and claims. 3. Describe what is known, what is uncertain, and why it matters. 4. Publish the sources so you can check our work.

What We Do Not Claim

Supreme Beauty does not label products "safe" or "unsafe" in an absolute sense. Risk depends on formulation, concentration, how often and how a product is used, and individual circumstances. We evaluate products against our evidence-based criteria and communicate the strength and limitations of the available evidence.
