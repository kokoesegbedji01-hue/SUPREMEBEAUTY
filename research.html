How we review research

Every Supreme Research article cites peer-reviewed journal articles, clinical guidelines, or systematic reviews. We state the study type (randomized trial, cohort study, narrative review), name the population studied, and separate what the evidence supports from what it does not. We do not turn a single observational finding into a product claim. Clinicians, researchers, and reviewers who find an error or have a better source are invited to contact us through the Join form, and we will correct the page.
