Who We Want To Meet

Salon and studio owners (hair, nails, lashes, barbering, skincare), beauty supply and retail store owners, and independent product brands that already have a loyal customer base.

A shared brand customers recognize, a research-first approach that builds trust, a community that talks about and posts your work, and a team focused on growing the whole experience: products, services, and people.

The Supreme Standard

Locations and brands that join are held to the Supreme Standard: quality products and services you can trust.
